Research Projects

Multi-Hazard Risk and Vulnerability Assessment

Chamba, Himachal Pradesh, India

  • Conducted a field-based multi-hazard assessment across 8–10 villages in Seismic Zone V, combining household surveys (n=50), satellite imagery analysis, and stakeholder interviews with district officials.
  • Processed Landsat imagery in QGIS to generate LULC classification maps (2020–21 vs. 2024–25) and NDVI change-detection maps (2015 vs. 2024–25) to quantify vegetation decline linked to deforestation and climate stress.
  • Found that 63% of surveyed households lacked reliable disaster early warning systems and 45% reported visible slope instability, aligning recommendations with NDMA and UNDRR Sendai Framework priorities.
  • Produced an NDBI map (2025) to evaluate built-up area expansion for DDMA-level policy planning across landslide, GLOF, cloudburst, and forest fire zones.

Ecological Sustainability Assessment

Gokarna, Karnataka, India

  • Designed and administered a mixed-methods survey across 6 thematic domains (agriculture, water quality, fishing, religious sites, coastal management, and women's safety) covering 80–100 respondents.
  • Created a supervised LULC classification map of Gokarna (2025) using QGIS to identify land cover dynamics across coastal, agricultural, and built-up zones.
  • Highlighted critical findings: 84.2% of fishers reported declining catches, 81.6% of farmers relied on borewell irrigation amidst a dropping water table, and a clear behavior-intention gap existed regarding coastal plastic waste.
